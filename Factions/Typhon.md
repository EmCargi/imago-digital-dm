# Typhon — The Neomorph House

**Type:** Megacorp — genetic engineering
**Products:** Genetically modified crops → clone-tank **neomorphs** (chimera pets), the **Neo-Evolution Grand Prix**
**Doctrine:** Consumer demand guides the breed; prizes include ultra-rare limited-edition neomorphs

Typhon pioneered neomorphs — adorable talking chimera fusions adopted by children and teens, incubated from neogenesis eggs over weeks. The Neo-Evolution Grand Prix is the sponsored organised play-battle circuit (neighbourhood → city → continental → world levels). Lately, rumoured stranger creatures — mythical beasts, demons, angels (the source: private wayline contacts).
