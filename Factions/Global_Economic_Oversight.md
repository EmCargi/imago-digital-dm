# Global Economic Oversight (GEO)

**Type:** World government
**Arm:** The World Police — investigative intelligence + security
**Doctrine:** Coordinate regions + corporations; suppress organised crime, data piracy, forbidden technology

GEO runs the comfortable Imago — universal basic income, abolished war, corporate oversight of fault zones. The World Police are its muscle, hunting criminals and (quietly) other-world intrusions. Some senior GEO leaders know the Cosmic Web is real.
