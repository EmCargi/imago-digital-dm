# The Fault-Zone Gangs & Mutant Gangs

**Type:** Organised crime — street gangs, fault-zone gangs, secret front corporations
**Product:** High-tech designer drugs — chiefly **Xyconal** (mutagenic super-steroid)
**Doctrine:** Power in the gaps the megacorps' oversight leaves

Crime families and gangs run the fault zones' shadow economy. The **mutant gangs** abuse Xyconal — enhanced neomorphs, monstrous "monster-outs," and gangs that spike their own growth. The World Police can't fully stamp them out.

**Cast hook:** **Rin** (fault-zone fixer, `[Credential: Fixer]`) knows the Xyconal trade — and where the Bazaroth demons slipped in (tie to Disc 9).
