# Warden Ona

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Global Economic Oversight — World Police
**Role:** Contrarian enforcer — the intrusion-response team
**Age:** 44

## Appearance

Formidable in a GEO Storm exo-suit, AR plating across the shoulders. The World Police rail she watches from is the highest perch in the arena. She's seen the Cosmic Web's bleed — and she's here to stop it.

## Personality

Unbending, thorough, jurisdiction-mad. The World Police's special intrusion-response team is her whole art — demons from Bazaroth, gate-breaches, forbidden technology. The law is the line between Imago and the Multiverse.

## Background

A decade on the intrusion team, hunting Bazaroth demons in the fault zones and Ikarion gate-jumpers. When the Grand Prix's monster-out spikes the server, she's the first to know the Ikarion gate is involved.

## Abilities

Exo-suit (GEO Storm gauntlet), VR-interface (World Police Nexus). Techniques: Judge Opponent, Precise Aim. Skills: Military 3, Street 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Jurisdiction's Rigidity — the line between worlds is also the line between people.
- **Sixth Guard:** Gate-Order Override — a confirmed gate-breach → she seals the perimeter over everything, even the party inside it.
- **Levers:** Storm-Interdict / Seal-Order / Nexus-Trace
