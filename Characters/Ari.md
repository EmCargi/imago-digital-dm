# Ari

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Monad — Nexus / Ikarion
**Role:** Gatekeeper/lore — built the Ikarion gate
**Age:** 36

## Appearance

Pale from server-room light, Brainscape contacts glinting, a Monad badge clipped to a jumpsuit. Fingers that have touched the virtual gate's code more times than anyone alive.

## Personality

Precise, withdrawn, mission-bound. Built the Ikarion gate — the section of the 28M-player game that's actually a wayline to Ikaris. Knows the breach is his fault and his to fix.

## Background

A Monad senior engineer who found the wayline by accident and quietly built the gate around it. The World Police's intrusion team knows; the megacorps suspect. When the arena's monster-out spikes the server, the gate flickers — and Ari is the only one who can read it.

## Abilities

VR-interface (Brainscape deep), Gate-sense (Ikarion/Ikaris). Techniques: Steady Hand, Evasion. Skills: Technical 3, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Engineer's Burden — the gate is his creation and his wound.
- **Sixth Guard:** Gate-Sever — forced to sever the Ikarion wayline → he destroys the game-world he built, and a part of himself.
- **Levers:** Code-Read / Gate-Step / Server-Veil
