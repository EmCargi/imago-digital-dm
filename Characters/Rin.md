# Rin

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Fault-Zone Gangs — fixer
**Role:** Neutral fixer — knows the Xyconal trade
**Age:** 31

## Appearance

Slight, grease-and-neon, a fault-zone jacket with too many pockets. Knows every exo-suit gang and every Xyconal vendor in the zone. Moves like the black market's favourite courier.

## Personality

Wry, greedy, loyal to the trade. The fault zone is home — the gangs, the mutants, the Bazaroth demons slipping between towers. Wants the monster-out quiet because a quiet zone is a safe zone.

## Background

Born in a fault zone, ran the Xyconal black market for a decade before it went too far. Knows who spiked the neomorph, where the exo-suit gang's lair is, and which World Police warden is on the take. The trail into the zone is hers to run.

## Abilities

VR-interface (Nexus black market), Exo-suit (scrapper hand). Techniques: Concealment, Portable Armoury. Skills: Street 3, Social 2, Technical 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Trade's Loyalty — the black market is her family.
- **Sixth Guard:** Zone-Burn — the fault zone is about to be sealed/carpet-bombed → she abandons the pursuit to save her people.
- **Levers:** Fixer's Route / Black-Market Pass / Zone-Slip
