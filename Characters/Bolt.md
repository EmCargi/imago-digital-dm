# Bolt

**Race:** Neomorph — a Xyconal'd chimera (talking, kitten-sized, monster-out prone)
**Faction:** Typhon — Kai Voss's star pet
**Role:** Moral thrall — redeem-or-scrap
**Age:** 1 (incubated)

## Appearance

A kitten-sized tyrannosaurus chimera — adorable, scaled, big-eyed. But the Xyconal in its growth-curve makes it flicker: sometimes a beloved pet, sometimes a towering monster-out. The arena's star and its threat.

## Personality

Loyal, playful, terrified of its own growth. Knows it was spiked — the Xyconal isn't its fault. Wants to be a good pet, but the monster-out is a reflex it can't always control. The party's choice: save it (the Trainer's bond) or scrap it (the World Police's order).

## Background

Kai Voss's champion, bred for the Grand Prix and secretly Xyconal'd by a fault-zone gang to rig the final. Its growth-curve is a bomb. When it monsters out, it doesn't want to — but it can't stop.

## Abilities

Neomorph instinct (fight/track), Growth-spike (monster-out). Techniques: Brutal, Lethal Blow. Defects: Xyconal (monster-out), Fragile (small).

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Growth-Curve — the Xyconal is a bomb inside it.
- **Sixth Guard:** Monster-Out — the growth spikes uncontrollably → it becomes the towering beast until overwhelmed, and the World Police order stands.
- **Levers:** Pet-Bond / Growth-Hold / Desperate Roar
