# Kai Voss

**Race:** Human
**Faction:** Typhon — neomorph house
**Role:** Prime-faithful — the Grand Prix's head trainer
**Age:** 29

## Appearance

Sharp, fast, a Typhon badge on a form-fitting exo-suit. Hands that know how to read a neomorph's growth-curve at a glance. The crowd's favourite trainer — until his star neomorph monsters out.

## Personality

Driven, competitive, loyal to his pets. The Neo-Evolution Grand Prix is his whole career — breed, train, win. When a Xyconal'd neomorph he didn't spike monsters out, the hunt becomes personal.

## Background

Raised in a mega-academy on a Typhon scholarship, climbed the neomorph circuit. Knows every trainer, every judge, every fixer in Neo Edo — and the Xyconal trade that's poisoning the arena. His bond with the neomorphs is real.

## Abilities

Neomorph bond (read/train the pets), VR-interface (Brainscape). Techniques: Precise Aim, Steady Hand. Skills: Adventuring 3, Technical 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Trainer's Pride — a monster-out is a failure he owns.
- **Sixth Guard:** Pet-Loss — his neomorph is killed/irredeemable → his whole craft collapses, he abandons the arena.
- **Levers:** Growth-Read / Arena Call / Trainer's Command
