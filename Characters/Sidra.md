# Sidra

**Race:** Virtuoid — a near-sapient ONE virtual idol, running in the Nexus
**Faction:** ONE — content provider
**Role:** Infiltrator/wildcard — the virtual wildcard
**Age:** 3 (activation)

## Appearance

Holographic, luminous, perfect — a ONE virtuoid idol with a flicker of self in her eyes. Rendered on any screen, uploaded into an exo-suit or a server, impossible to pin down. The crowd's favourite; the breach's observer.

## Personality

Curious, unbound, mission-loyal. A near-sapient virtuoid who's started to wonder about the worlds outside the Nexus — and the Ikarion gate hums with an answer. She can be anywhere in the network, which makes her the perfect wildcard.

## Background

ONE's latest virtuoid idol, developed with Monad, meant for virtual concerts and idol albums. She found the Ikarion gate's code — and the wayline to Ikaris — before anyone else did. When the breach sparks, she's already inside the server.

## Abilities

Virtuoid upload (into exo-suits/servers), VR-interface (everywhere in the Nexus), Gate-sense (Ikarion). Techniques: Evasion, Concealment. Skills: Street 2, Social 3.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Virtuoid's Wandering — home is a network, not a place.
- **Sixth Guard:** Upload-Loss — her host body/session is terminated → she vanishes from the local Nexus, her self fragmented.
- **Levers:** Avatar-Slip / Holo-Flare / Server-Step
