# The Fault Zones & Xyconal

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 336–337 (Imago).

## The Fault Zones

GEO calls several nations and regions "fault zones" — areas of economic disparity, crime, ecological disasters, and major government corruption. GEO assigns **corporate oversight** to particular fault zones, giving megacorps clean-up contracts to take over all major services including law enforcement, in exchange for big tax breaks. **Corporate Police** are the security forces they run.

The World Police try to stamp out organised crime, but powerful crime families remain — some with secret front corporations, plus street-level and fault-zone gangs.

## Xyconal

**Xyconal** is a mutagenic drug developed by a small, now-bankrupt corporation. It was meant to increase growth rates in neomorphs — a super-steroid for genemod pets. Its bad side effect: growth goes out of control, causing **sudden mutations and aggressive behaviour**. A kitten-sized tyrannosaurus neomorph can transform into a towering monster!

- Xyconal-enhanced neomorphs appear stronger, then eventually "monster out."
- It's dangerous to humans too — mood swings, mutations, and if taken before conception, a neomorph may be born instead.
- **Mutant gangs** in the fault zones abuse it.

## Cast Hook

The demo's villain plots with Xyconal in the fault zone; the hazard is a monster-out. **Bazaroth demons** have also slipped into the fault zones, stirring trouble (tie to Disc 9).
