# The Megacorps — Typhon, Monad, ONE, & More

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 336–337 (Imago).

## The Corporate Landscape

The major powers besides GEO are giant multinational conglomerates with incomes vaster than any nation. Five anchor the world:

| Corp | Business | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Typhon** | Genetically modified crops, then **neomorphs** (clone-tank chimera pets), Neo-Evolution Grand Prix | The demo's villain-adjacent (a Xyconal'd neomorph) |
| **Monad** | The **Nexus** (vast computer network), VR operating systems, **Brainscape** gear, **Ikarion** (28M-player VR fantasy world) | The gate to Ikaris lives here |
| **ONE** | Content provider — 3-D movies, games, pop stars, near-sapient **virtuoids** | The wildcard (a virtuoid) |
| **Exodynamics** | Robots + powered exoskeletons (exo-mechs) — GEO Storm police, Meteor Ball | The signature asset |
| **Brainsponge** | Alternative news/music/media, rivals ONE | The fixer's source |
| **Spectrum Heavy Industries (SHI)** | Autos, aircraft, power plants, engines, heavy industry + exo-mech line | Flavor |

## The Academies

Many children attend school through the Nexus. Special **mega-academies** (ONE Academy, Monad School of Technology) are sponsored by the megacorps, with distinctive uniforms and fierce rivalry. Fees are high; scholarships are won through competitions.

**Kai Voss** (Typhon neomorph-trainer), **Ari** (Monad engineer who built the Ikarion gate), **Sidra** (ONE virtuoid).
