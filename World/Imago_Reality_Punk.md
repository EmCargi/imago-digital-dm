# Imago — The Reality-Punk World

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 336–337 (Imago).

## Overview

Imago is a world much like Earth but somewhat more advanced. Globalisation has led to a unified basic standard of living (though richer and poorer regions remain). Technology ranges from early-21st-century-equivalent to breakthroughs in **robotics, powered exoskeletons, genetic engineering, and virtual reality**.

National divisions are largely abolished. Major urban mega-cities include **Kwalur, Meridian, Newport, and Neo Edo**, each with populations of several million. There is a world government, **Global Economic Oversight (GEO)**, coordinating relations between regions and corporations.

## The Comfortable World

War is abolished and a basic level of prosperity is provided across the world — driven largely by a highly successful **universal basic income**. The big profits go to consumer and entertainment sectors. Fearing crime and unemployment, GEO and the megacorps channel aggressiveness into competition: rival factions challenge one another through energetic (and sometimes violent) sports and games.

## The Shadow

The comfort is shallow. **Fault zones** — regions of economic disparity, crime, ecological disaster, and corruption — are assigned corporate oversight. The **World Police** hunt international crime, data pirates, and forbidden technology. And under the surface, the fault zones breed Xyconal mutant gangs, Bazaroth demons, and the Ikarion gate.
