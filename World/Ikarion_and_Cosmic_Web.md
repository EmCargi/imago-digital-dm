# Ikarion & The Cosmic Web — The Virtual Gate

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 337 (Imago).

## Ikarion

**Ikarion** is a massively multiplayer virtual reality fantasy world on the Nexus with over **28 million players**. Using Monad's **Brainscape** gear, players interact with the Nexus as if in reality, creating avatar bodies. Some schools spend much of their time in the Nexus.

## The Virtual Gate

**One section of the Ikarion game is actually a "virtual gate" to the Prime World of Ikaris.** Players who reach certain levels may be accidentally (or deliberately!) transported to the real world of Ikaris. The World Police have a special team devoted to dealing with intrusions (demons, etc.) from other worlds.

## The Cosmic Web

Imago has **seven waylines**: one to Earth, two to Inner Worlds, and four to all the other Prime Worlds save Cathedral. Some senior GEO leaders are aware of the Cosmic Web. The megacorps and gangs have private wayline contacts — the source of the genetic material Typhon uses to clone dragons, angels, and other mythical "limited edition" neomorphs.

## Cast Hook

The demo's lore node is the **Ikarion server room** — the virtual gate humming, the tie to Disc 7 (Ikaris). The breach is real.
