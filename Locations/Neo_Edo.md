# Neo Edo — The Grand Prix Arena

**Type:** Mega-city — home of the Neo-Evolution Grand Prix
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's hub (`node_01_neo_edo`)

Neo Edo is one of Imago's several-million-strong mega-cities. The demo opens at the Neo-Evolution Grand Prix — the sponsored neomorph battle circuit's city level — a gleaming arena ringed by GEO, World Police, and megacorp booths (Typhon, Monad, ONE).

**Map:**

```
[node_01_neo_edo] → [node_02_press] → [node_03_arena] → [node_04_ikarion]
                                                          ↘ [node_05_fault_zone]
                                                                ↘ [node_06_server]
```

**Validates as:** `node_type: entrance` — the starting node of `ikarion_breach.json`.
