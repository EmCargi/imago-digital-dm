# Exo-Suits & Neomorph Bond

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 336–337 (Imago).

## Exo-Suits

Powered exoskeletons are ubiquitous — Exodynamics and SHI compete. GEO Storm police use form-fitting exo-mechs; Meteor Ball teams play in them; second-hand suits cost about a used car; street gangs upgrade them with power-skate and drill add-ons. Banned in fault-zone corporate-police use: tanks, fighter jets, heavy vehicles.

## Neomorphs

Clone-tank chimera pets — pint-sized dinosaur replicas, adorable talking animal fusions, rumoured mythical beasts. Incubated from neogenesis eggs over weeks. The Neo-Evolution Grand Prix is their battle circuit. Xyconal makes them monster out.

## Powers → card_json

Neomorph bond, VR-interface (Brainscape), Virtuoid upload, Gate-sense (Ikarion) all have **no DB column** — they live in `card_json.powers` + the `race` column as narrative, injected as prose (the established Enid/Cathedral/Ikaris/Aradia/Bazaroth pattern). The loadout DB columns carry techniques/skills/defects + Narrative Syntax.
